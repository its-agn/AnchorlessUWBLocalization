# Synthetic UWB ToF test cases

Ten time-series test cases of simulated DWM3001C two-way-ranging data for the A01
anchorless localization project. They were generated by `../generate_uwb_test_cases.py`;
re-running that script reproduces these files exactly.

## Setup common to all cases

- **Nodes:** 5 nodes (`N1`-`N5`) on a flat 10 x 10 m floor, all mounted at z = 1.0 m, so
  the problem is effectively 2D. The farthest pair is always <= 15 m apart, so every valid
  ToF is <= 50050 ps (~3198 DTU).
- **Ranging:** DS-TWR on channel 5. Each pair gets one 10 ms TDMA slot in a 100 ms cycle,
  so every pair is ranged at 10 Hz and each node makes 40 ranges/s. The initiator
  (`source_id`) alternates every cycle.
- **Timestamps:** `timestamp_us` is microseconds since the start of the run, on a shared
  (already synchronized) clock.
- **`seq`:** a per-source-node counter. It restarts at 0 when a node reboots (TC09).

## Files in each case folder

| File | Contents |
|------|----------|
| `measurements.jsonl` | One ranging attempt per line. This is the pipeline input. |
| `labels.jsonl` | Truth for the same line number in `measurements.jsonl`: `true_distance_m`, `condition` (`los`/`nlos`), `blockers`, `nlos_bias_m`, `fault`. |
| `ground_truth.csv` | True `x_m, y_m, z_m` of every node at 10 Hz. |
| `scenario.json` | Layout, obstacles, offline windows, expected behaviour and generated statistics. |

Keep `labels.jsonl` away from the system under test. Use it only for scoring.

## Measurement record

```json
{"seq": 0, "timestamp_us": 1632, "source_id": "N1", "target_id": "N2", "status": "ok", "tof_ps": 15685.6, "tof_dtu": 1002, "distance_m": 4.701, "diag": {"channel": 5, "rssi_dbm": -74.7, "fp_power_dbm": -77.2, "noise_std": 46.1, "quality": 0.94}}
```

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `status` | `ok`, `timeout` (no response) or `rx_error` (corrupted frame). Failed attempts have `null` ToF, distance and diagnostics. |
| `tof_ps` | One-way time of flight in picoseconds. |
| `tof_dtu` | The same ToF in DW3000 device time units (1 DTU = 1/(128 x 499.2 MHz) ~ 15.65 ps). |
| `distance_m` | `tof_ps` x c_air, with c_air = 299,702,547 m/s. |
| `diag.rssi_dbm` | Estimated total received power. |
| `diag.fp_power_dbm` | First-path power. RSSI minus FP power is < 6 dB for LOS and > 10 dB for NLOS. |
| `diag.noise_std` | CIR noise standard deviation (accumulator units). |
| `diag.quality` | 0-1 confidence derived from SNR and the FP/RSSI gap. |

## Error model

- **LOS:** a fixed per-pair antenna-delay bias, U(+/-4 cm), plus Gaussian noise with
  sigma = 4 cm + 0.4 cm per metre of range.
- **NLOS:** an extra positive bias that depends on the material (person 0.2-0.5 m,
  drywall 0.3-0.8 m, concrete 0.6-1.5 m), positively skewed noise, 6-15 dB extra
  attenuation, and a higher timeout probability.
- **RSSI:** -63 dBm at 1 m with path-loss exponent 2, plus 1.5 dB shadowing.
- **Timeouts:** ~0.5% at short range, rising to ~3% at 14 m.

### TC01_static_baseline_los: Static baseline, line of sight

Five stationary nodes on a regular pentagon (radius 4 m, centred at 5,5). All links are line of sight. Use this case to measure the raw ranging noise floor and per-pair bias, and to verify basic multilateration.

Expected:
- Every link LOS; errors ~N(pair bias, 4-7 cm)
- Localization should recover the pentagon (up to rotation/reflection) within ~0.1 m
- Estimated positions should stay still

### TC02_static_long_range: Static, long range near the 15 m limit

Four nodes in the corners of the floor and one off-centre. The diagonals are about 13.5 m, close to the 15 m limit. Range noise grows with distance, RSSI drops to about -86 dBm, and timeouts are more frequent on the longest links.

Expected:
- Long-link sigma ~9-10 cm vs ~6 cm on short links
- Timeout rate ~2-3% on 13 m+ links
- Confidence weighting by range/RSSI should improve the fit

### TC03_single_mover: One moving node, four static

N1-N4 stand still on the corners of an 8 x 8 m square. After 2 s, N5 walks a 4 x 4 m rectangle inside the square at 1.0 m/s and loops it for the rest of the run.

Expected:
- Static nodes should stay put
- N5 track should trace a 4 x 4 m rectangle at 1 m/s

### TC04_all_moving_random: All five nodes moving

Every node follows a smoothed pedestrian-like random walk at 0.3-1.2 m/s. Nodes stay inside the floor and steer away from each other. All links are line of sight.

Expected:
- All pairwise ranges change continuously
- Cooperative localization should track every node; compare to ground_truth.csv

### TC05_rigid_group_motion: Whole group moves as a rigid body

The five nodes form a compact, slightly irregular formation (~3 m across) that moves as one rigid body. It holds still for 3 s, translates 6 m in +x at 0.8 m/s, pauses for 2 s, rotates 30 deg about its centre over 5 s, then holds. Pairwise distances never change, so the ranges look identical to a static scene. This is the documented failure mode that the IMU is meant to fix.

Expected:
- Ranges are statistically stationary for the whole run
- UWB-only localization cannot see the translation/rotation; it should report a static shape (or flag reduced observability)
- Ground truth shows 6 m translation + 30 deg rotation

### TC06_parallel_pair: Two nodes walking side by side

N3-N5 are static. After 2 s, N1 and N2 walk the same loop side by side, with N2 always 1.5 m in +y from N1, at 1.0 m/s. The N1-N2 range stays constant at 1.5 m, but their ranges to the static nodes change, so the motion is observable.

Expected:
- N1-N2 measured range constant ~1.5 m
- N1/N2 tracks should be parallel and 1.5 m apart
- Contrast with TC05, where no static node is present

### TC07_collinear_degenerate: Near-collinear geometry, then recovery

All five nodes start almost on the line y = 5, with at most 5 cm lateral offset. This geometry is degenerate: the 2D solution is ill-conditioned and has a reflection ambiguity across the line. At t = 12 s, N4 walks 2 m off the line at 1 m/s, after which the geometry is well conditioned.

Expected:
- Before 12 s: solver should flag poor geometry (high GDOP / low confidence)
- Lateral position of nodes is essentially unobservable before 12 s
- After ~14 s: estimates converge

### TC08_nlos_obstruction: NLOS: wall, pillar and a walking person

Five static nodes. A drywall partition permanently blocks N1-N2, and a concrete pillar permanently blocks N4-N5. From t = 3 s, a person walks back and forth along x = 7 and briefly blocks N1-N2, N1-N3, N2-N4, N2-N5, N3-N4 and N3-N5 as they cross. NLOS ranges read long (+0.2 to +1.5 m), with higher variance and lower RSSI. Their RSSI minus first-path power gap is above 10 dB, against under 6 dB for LOS. labels.jsonl marks every NLOS measurement and its blockers.

Expected:
- N1-N2 and N4-N5 biased long for the whole run
- Short NLOS bursts on links the person crosses
- RSSI - FP power > 10 dB flags NLOS (non-ML baseline)
- NLOS-aware weighting should reduce position error vs. naive LS

### TC09_dropouts_and_recovery: Packet loss, burst outage and a node dropout

All nodes are moving. On top of the normal timeouts there is 5% random packet loss, and the N2-N3 link fails completely from 6 s to 8 s. N4 powers off from 10 s to 14 s: it sends no reports, and other nodes ranging to it record timeouts. When N4 comes back it has rebooted, so its seq counter restarts at 0.

Expected:
- System tolerates missing ranges without diverging (MVP 1.d)
- N2-N3 link expires after 6 s and is restored at 8 s
- N4 estimate marked degraded/stale during 10-14 s, recovers automatically (MVP 1.e)
- Pipeline handles N4 seq reset to 0 after reboot

### TC10_invalid_and_outliers: Corrupted, impossible and malformed packets

Slow motion: N2 walks toward N1 until they are 0.4 m apart, then back, and N5 drifts along the top edge. About 2% of records are corrupted with spikes (+3 to 8 m), negative ToF at close range, impossible ranges (18-60 m), missing fields, unknown or self-referencing node IDs, packets delivered 2-5 s late, swapped (out-of-order) packets and exact duplicates. Each fault is labelled in labels.jsonl. Apart from the labelled faults, every range is within (0, 15] m.

Expected:
- Ingestion rejects/flags every labelled fault (MVP 3.f)
- Negative and > 15 m ranges rejected as physically impossible
- Duplicates (same source_id + seq) dropped; stale packets flagged by age
- Localization output unaffected by the injected faults
